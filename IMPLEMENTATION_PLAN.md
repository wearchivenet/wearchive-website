# Astra Arcana Implementation Plan

## Product direction

A multilingual astrology and tarot practice focused on psychological insight, symbolic literacy, personal agency, and inclusive consultation.

## Visual thesis

Dark editorial mysticism crossed with the Progress Pride spectrum: near-black structure, luminous trans pink and blue, and high-energy rainbow accents. The design avoids generic purple gradients, crystal-ball clichés, and card-grid-heavy layouts.

## Motion thesis

1. A choreographed hero entrance with orbit and aurora depth.
2. Scroll-linked image parallax, spectrum movement, and reading progress.
3. Restrained reveal and hover motion that respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Information architecture

- Astrology consultations
- Tarot readings
- Celestial journal
- Inspiration library
- Booking
- About

All routes are available in Traditional Chinese, English, and French.
